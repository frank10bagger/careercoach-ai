# CareerTwin — 1-Page User Guide

**Live URL: https://careercoach-ai-phi.vercel.app**

---

## What it does

**CareerTwin is the digital twin for your career management.** Your fragmented career information — resumes, achievements, applications, coffee chats, networking notes, milestones — becomes one living memory layer. You enter your background once. Every AI feature reuses it. Built for undergrads, working professionals, and career switchers.

## How to sign up (15 seconds)

1. Open **https://careercoach-ai-phi.vercel.app**
2. Click **Get started**
3. Enter your full name, email, and a password (6+ characters)
4. You're instantly logged in — no email verification needed

## Four AI features. One persistent profile.

| Feature | What you give | What you get back |
|---|---|---|
| **Onboarding** (one-time, ~3 min) | Conversational 8-step Q&A: name, contact, current role, work history (multi-job), education, target role, target industry, skills + interests | A persona classification (Student / Professional / Career Switcher) and a saved profile that powers everything below |
| **Resume** | Click **Generate** | A one-page resume in MBA-alumni style. Edit inline, click **Publish** for a styled preview, click **Download PDF** for a one-page letter-format PDF |
| **Cover letter** | Company name, job title, full job description | A 350–450 word cover letter referencing both the JD and your stored resume. Copy to clipboard. |
| **Thank-you email** | After a coffee chat: contact name, role, company, debrief notes | A personalized thank-you email referencing the actual topics you discussed (not a template). Copy to clipboard. |
| **Career highlights** | One sentence about a milestone (e.g. *"Promoted to Senior Consultant at Bain"*) | A polished LinkedIn post + a resume bullet. The bullet auto-flows into your next resume generation. |

## Privacy

Your data is yours alone — every database query is filtered by your user ID at the database level (Postgres Row Level Security). Sign in 6 months later and everything is still there.
